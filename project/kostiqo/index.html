PROJECT 02 — PROPERTY & DIGITAL SYSTEM

Kostiqo

A boarding house business concept focused on creating a simple digital presence, presenting the property clearly, and exploring how technology can support everyday management.

Type Property Project
Focus Digital Management
Status Developing

The Idea

Kostiqo is a boarding house business concept built around the idea of combining a physical property with a simple and useful digital experience.

The goal is to make information about the property easier to access while also exploring how digital tools could help simplify everyday management.

What I Built

The project includes a website concept and ideas for connecting the property with digital management systems.

  • Kostiqo landing page
  • Property information system
  • Room information
  • Digital management concept
  • QR-based room identification concept
  • Notion-based management possibilities

Technology

Kostiqo gave me an opportunity to explore how a relatively simple property website could eventually connect to a larger digital management system.

HTML CSS JavaScript Google Maps Notion QR Code Web Development

What I Learned

Kostiqo taught me that a website for a business does not have to be complicated to be useful.

Good information structure, clear presentation, simple navigation, and a practical connection between the physical business and its digital presence can make a significant difference.

The project also gave me ideas about how websites, databases, QR codes, and management tools could eventually work together.

“A simple digital experience can make a physical business easier to understand.”
