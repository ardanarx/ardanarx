PROJECT 01 — BUSINESS & DIGITAL SYSTEM

Vervocare

A laundry business project where I explored how technology and digital systems could be used to simplify everyday operations and create a more organized way of working.

Type Business Project
Focus Digital Systems
Status Active / Developing

The Idea

Vervocare started as a business idea around laundry services. As the project developed, I became interested not only in the business itself, but also in how technology could help manage the everyday work behind it.

Instead of relying entirely on manual processes, I explored ways to build simple digital systems that could make information easier to record, organize, and access.

What I Built

One of the main parts of the project was creating a digital attendance system for managing staff attendance.

  • Digital attendance system
  • Clock-in and clock-out workflow
  • Attendance records
  • Monthly attendance recap
  • Progressive Web App interface
  • Digital business management experiments

Technology

The project became an opportunity to experiment with several technologies and understand how different services could work together as one system.

HTML CSS JavaScript Google Apps Script Google Sheets Notion API PWA QR Code

What I Learned

Building Vervocare taught me that creating a digital system is not only about writing code. The system has to match the way people actually work.

I learned about connecting different services, handling data, designing simple workflows, troubleshooting unexpected problems, and gradually improving a system as new requirements appear.

It also showed me that even a relatively small business can benefit from thoughtful digital systems when they are designed around real everyday needs.

“The goal was never to build something complicated. It was to build something useful.”
